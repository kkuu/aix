Sleep Hush Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Sleep Hush (hereinafter referred to as "we", "our" or "this app") highly values your privacy and personal information protection. This Privacy Policy is intended to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Important Notice: Please carefully read and understand this Privacy Policy before using this app. If you disagree with any content of this Privacy Policy, please stop using this app immediately.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Email Address: When you contact us for feedback or customer support, we may collect your email address
  • Favorites: Your list of favorited sounds and audio
  • Mixer Presets: Custom mixer configurations you create and save
  • App Settings: Your preference settings such as volume settings and timer configurations
  • Purchase Records: Premium membership plan type, transaction ID, and purchase status (used for subscription verification)

1.2 Information We Automatically Collect

  • Crash Logs: When the app crashes, we collect crash logs to diagnose and fix issues
  • Diagnostic Information: Including device model, operating system version, app version, memory usage, etc.
  • App Performance Data: Including app startup time, response time, feature usage, playback behavior, etc.
  • Device ID: Anonymous identifier used to identify unique devices for abuse prevention and statistical analysis
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen size
  • App Usage Information: Feature usage, ad viewing frequency, playback behavior
  • Network Information: IP address, network type, connection status

1.3 Recording and Sleep-Monitoring Audio (Microphone Permission)

Some features of this app require the device microphone (RECORD_AUDIO permission) to capture audio:

  • Sleep Monitoring: To analyze your sleep and snoring, when you start monitoring the app records ambient audio throughout the night via a foreground service (MICROPHONE type). This audio is used solely for on-device snore detection and sleep analysis, and all processing is completed locally on your device.
  • Recording: You may actively record audio; the resulting files are stored locally on your device.
  • Editing: You may edit your recorded audio (e.g., trim, rename); the edited results are also stored only on your device.
Important Notice (Microphone Data): The audio captured via the microphone, as well as the data generated by sleep monitoring, is stored only on your device, is never uploaded to any server, is never shared with any third party, and is never used for advertising or any commercial marketing purposes. All analysis and processing are performed locally on your device; the developer cannot access your recordings.

2.1 Service Provision

2.2 Advertising Services

  • Deliver personalized or general advertisements
  • Limit ad display frequency
  • Measure advertising effectiveness

2.3 Feature Optimization

  • Analyze app usage to improve features
  • Remotely configure app behavior and content
  • Fix crashes and technical issues

2.4 Recording and Sleep Monitoring

  • When you actively start sleep monitoring, record audio through the microphone for snore detection and sleep-quality analysis
  • Provide audio recording for local saving and playback on your device
  • Provide local editing (trim, rename) of recordings
  • All analysis and processing of the above audio is performed locally on your device—no upload, no external transmission

4. Data Storage

4.1 Local Storage

  • We use device local storage (DataStore) to save your preference settings, favorites list, and mixer presets
  • Cached audio files are stored in device local storage
  • Recording and Sleep-Monitoring Audio: Audio you actively record, as well as the all-night recordings generated during sleep monitoring, are stored only in your device's local file system (the app's private directory) and are never uploaded to any cloud server

4.2 Cloud Storage

  • Your favorites and mixer preset data are stored in Firebase backend services
  • Online audio content is stored in Firebase Storage
  • Audio from recording and sleep monitoring is NOT part of cloud storage: this data stays only on your device and is never sent to Firebase or any other cloud

4.3 Data Retention

  • Unless required or permitted by law to retain data for a longer period, we will retain your data for some time after you delete your account or stop using the service
  • Locally cached audio can be cleared in app settings
  • Purchase Status: Your membership subscription status (including plan type and validity) is stored locally and verified through Google Play

5. Data Security

  • HTTPS encryption for all network communications
  • Anonymization of device identifiers
  • Limited employee access to user data
  • Regular review of data collection and storage practices
