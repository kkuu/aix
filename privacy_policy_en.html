Sleep Hush (hereinafter referred to as "we", "our" or "this app") highly values your privacy and personal information protection. This Privacy Policy is intended to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Important Notice: Please carefully read and understand this Privacy Policy before using this app. If you disagree with any content of this Privacy Policy, please stop using this app immediately.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You主动提供

1.2 Information We Automatically Collect

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.2 Advertising Services

  • Deliver personalized or general advertisements

  • Measure advertising effectiveness

2.3 Feature Optimization

  • Analyze app usage to improve features
  • Remotely configure app behavior and content

4. Data Storage

4.1 Local Storage

  • We use device local storage (DataStore) to save your preference settings, favorites list, and mixer presets
  • Cached audio files are stored in device local storage

4.2 Cloud Storage

  • Your favorites and mixer preset data are stored in Firebase backend services
  • Online audio content is stored in Firebase Storage

4.3 Data Retention

  • Unless required or permitted by law to retain data for a longer period, we will retain your data for some time after you delete your account or stop using the service
  • Locally cached audio can be cleared in app settings

5. Data Security

We employ the following security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all network communications
  • Anonymization of device identifiers
  • Limited employee access to user data
  • Regular review of data collection and storage practices

6.2 Deletion

6.3 Withdraw Consent

6.4 Data Control
